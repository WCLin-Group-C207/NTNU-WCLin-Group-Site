01

Magneto-optical Kerr Microscope

Magnetic Domain Imaging
Gas Environment Variable Temperature

Wide-field magneto-optical Kerr imaging that directly visualizes magnetic domain structure and domain-wall dynamics in real time, enabling immediate observation of magnetization reversal and hysteresis behavior.

This instrument is also shared with NTNU's Instrumentation Center.

03

Vibrating Sample Magnetometer

Magnetic Property Measurement
Programmable Variable Temperature

Quantitative measurement of a sample's magnetic moment as a function of applied field and temperature, used to characterize hysteresis loops, saturation magnetization, and Curie temperature.

This instrument is co-managed with Prof. Fang-Yuh Lo's group and shared with NTNU's Instrumentation Center.
