Instrumentation

Facilities

01

Magneto-optical Kerr Microscope

Magnetic Domain Imaging
Gas Environment Variable Temperature

Real-space imaging of magnetic domain structure and domain-wall dynamics using the magneto-optical Kerr effect, enabling direct visualization of magnetization reversal and hysteresis behavior.

This instrument is also shared with NTNU's Instrumentation Center.

01

Magneto-optical Kerr Microscope

Magnetic Domain Imaging

Real-space imaging of magnetic domain structure and domain-wall dynamics using the magneto-optical Kerr effect, enabling direct visualization of magnetization reversal and hysteresis behavior.

This instrument is also shared with NTNU's Instrumentation Center.

Modes: Polar / Longitudinal / Transverse Kerr
Applications: domain imaging, M–H loops, time-resolved diffusion mapping
02

Atomic Force Microscope

Surface Characterization

High-resolution imaging of surface topography at the nanometer scale by scanning a sharp probe across the sample surface, revealing morphology, roughness, and fine surface features.

02

Atomic Force Microscope

Surface Characterization

High-resolution imaging of surface topography at the nanometer scale by scanning a sharp probe across the sample surface, revealing morphology, roughness, and fine surface features.

Capabilities: Tapping / Contact / Conductive modes
Applications: bubble domain creation, PL patterning in 2D heterostructures
03

Vibrating Sample Magnetometer

Magnetic Property Measurement

Quantitative measurement of a sample's magnetic moment as a function of applied field and temperature, used to characterize hysteresis loops, saturation magnetization, and Curie temperature.

This instrument is co-managed with Prof. Fang-Yuh Lo's group and shared with NTNU's Instrumentation Center.

03

Vibrating Sample Magnetometer

Magnetic Property Measurement

Quantitative measurement of a sample's magnetic moment as a function of applied field and temperature, used to characterize hysteresis loops, saturation magnetization, and Curie temperature.

This instrument is co-managed with Prof. Fang-Yuh Lo's group and shared with NTNU's Instrumentation Center.

Field range: typically up to ±2 T
Temperature: variable (low-T option available)
04

UHV e-beam Evaporation

Thin-Film Deposition

Physical vapor deposition of thin films under ultra-high vacuum, using an electron beam to evaporate source materials for precise, contamination-free layer growth.

04

UHV e-beam Evaporation

Thin-Film Deposition

Physical vapor deposition of thin films under ultra-high vacuum, using an electron beam to evaporate source materials for precise, contamination-free layer growth.

Base pressure: 10⁻¹⁰ Torr range
Materials: Fe, Co, Ni, Pd, Pt, Cu, Mn, noble gases
05

Chemical Vapor Deposition

Materials Growth, Synthesis & Annealing

Growth of thin films and 2D materials through gas-phase chemical reactions on a heated substrate, along with post-growth annealing to control crystallinity and material quality.

This system is operated in collaboration with Prof. Fang-Yuh Lo's group.

05

Chemical Vapor Deposition

Materials Growth, Synthesis & Annealing

Growth of thin films and 2D materials through gas-phase chemical reactions on a heated substrate, along with post-growth annealing to control crystallinity and material quality.

This system is operated in collaboration with Prof. Fang-Yuh Lo's group.

Materials grown: Graphene, hBN, MoS₂, perovskite oxides
Substrates: SiO₂/Si, sapphire, transparent conductive oxides
06

Raman Spectrometer

Structural & Vibrational Analysis

Vibrational spectroscopy technique that probes molecular and lattice vibrations to identify material composition, crystal structure, strain, and layer number in low-dimensional materials.

This instrument is also shared with NTNU's Instrumentation Center.

06

Raman Spectrometer

Structural & Vibrational Analysis

Vibrational spectroscopy technique that probes molecular and lattice vibrations to identify material composition, crystal structure, strain, and layer number in low-dimensional materials.

This instrument is also shared with NTNU's Instrumentation Center.

Excitation: Multi-wavelength laser lines
Applications: 2D material layer counting, strain mapping, PL spectra
